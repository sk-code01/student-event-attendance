# Student Guide

## What this system does for you

It records the events you take part in, with evidence that you were actually
there, so that your attendance, on-duty leave and achievements can be approved
on the strength of that evidence.

Four things are separate, and each needs its own step:

| | Means |
|---|---|
| **Registration** | You said you intend to attend |
| **Participation** | You took a live photo at the event with GPS |
| **Attendance / OD approval** | Your HOD approved the record your Faculty requested |
| **Achievement** | Faculty recorded a result, and your HOD approved it |

Registering is not attending. Being verified is not attendance approval.
Attendance approval is not an achievement. Each is a deliberate decision by
someone.

---

## Creating your account

1. Open the application and choose **Register**.
2. Fill in your username, email, password, role (**Student**) and department.
3. Submit. **You cannot log in yet.**

Your request goes to your department's HOD. You will be able to log in once it
is approved.

**Checking your status** — from the login page you can check your registration
status with the username and email you registered with, without an account.
Possible answers: *pending* (waiting for your HOD), *approved* (log in now), or
*rejected* (with the reason your HOD gave).

**Username and email must be unique.** If yours is taken, choose another.

---

## Logging in

Use your username and password. If login fails you will be told which of these
applies:

- *Your account is pending HOD approval* — not reviewed yet.
- *Your registration request was rejected* — the reason is shown when you check
  your status.
- *This account has been deactivated* — contact your HOD.
- A generic failure means the username or password is wrong.

Your session refreshes automatically while you use the app. If you have been
away for a long time you may be asked to log in again.

**Log out when you finish on a shared device.** Log out is in the top-right
menu. It invalidates your session on the server; simply closing the tab does
not.

## Finding and registering for events

**Events** lists what is open to you. You can search and filter by category,
date and department.

Open an event to see the date, venue, category, conducting college, department
and the registration window. **Register** appears only when:

- the event is published, **and**
- today is inside the registration window, **and**
- you have not already registered.

If the button is missing, the event detail says why.

**Cancelling.** *My Registrations* lists everything you have registered for, and
lets you cancel. Cancelling before the event is normal and carries no penalty —
but you cannot capture participation evidence for an event you have cancelled.

## Participation: the live capture

**This is the important part, and it can only be done on the day of the event.**

### Before you start

| Requirement | Why |
|-------------|-----|
| You are registered for the event | Participation is only open to registrants |
| **It is the event date** | A capture on any other day is rejected |
| A working camera | The photo must be taken live, in the browser |
| Location enabled, with a good signal | GPS is mandatory |
| **HTTPS** (a padlock in the address bar) | Browsers only allow camera and GPS on a secure connection |

A phone outdoors or near a window gives the best GPS accuracy. A desktop with no
GPS hardware often cannot meet the accuracy requirement at all.

### Step by step

1. From the event (or *My Registrations*), choose **Capture participation**.
2. The app checks your eligibility first and tells you immediately if you cannot
   proceed — before asking for any permission.
3. **Allow camera access** when the browser asks.
4. **Allow location access** when the browser asks. Wait a moment while a fix is
   acquired; the accuracy is shown on screen.
5. Frame the shot so that **you and the event surroundings are both visible** —
   the venue, a banner, the stage, the crowd. Faculty reviews this image, and a
   close-up of your face against a blank wall gives them nothing to verify.
6. Press the shutter. Review the photo.
7. **Not happy? Retake.** There is no limit on retakes before you submit.
8. Optionally add more photos. One is required; more can only help.
9. Press **Submit**.

Your submission is complete when the app confirms the server accepted it. Until
you see that confirmation, it is not submitted.

### When something is refused

| Message | What to do |
|---------|-----------|
| *Camera access requires a secure connection* | The site is not on HTTPS. Report it — you cannot work around this. |
| *Camera access was denied* | Re-allow it: padlock icon → Site settings → Camera. |
| *Location access was denied* | Re-allow it the same way. Submission stays blocked until you do. |
| *This browser does not support camera access* | Use an up-to-date Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari. |
| *No camera was found* | The device has no camera the browser can use. |
| *The camera is already in use* | Close the other app using it. |
| *Location accuracy (N m) does not meet the required precision* | Move outside or near a window and wait for a better fix. Indoors on Wi-Fi location this often fails. |
| *This capture was not taken on the event date* | Captures are only valid on the event's own date. |
| *You are not registered for this event* | Register first — but only while registration is open. |

**There is no way around the camera or GPS requirement.** If you cannot satisfy
them, speak to your Faculty rather than looking for a workaround.

### A note on the venue warning

If your location is further from the venue than expected, the capture is still
accepted and submitted — but it is flagged for your Faculty to look at. A large
campus, a changed room or ordinary GPS drift can all cause this. It is
information for the reviewer, not a rejection.

### Capturing without a signal

If you lose connectivity, capture anyway. The photo is stored on your device and
uploads automatically when you are back online — keep the app open until it
confirms, and check *My Participation* afterwards to make sure it went through.

The photo's original time is kept, so a late upload of a same-day capture is
still valid. It does **not** work the other way round: uploading tomorrow a
photo taken tomorrow does not make it count for today's event.

## After you submit

**My Participation** shows every submission and its state:

| State | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| **Submitted** | Waiting for Faculty |
| **Under review** | Faculty is looking at it |
| **Verified** | Accepted. Attendance, OD and achievements can now follow. |
| **Rejected** | Not accepted. The reason is shown. |
| **Resubmission required** | Faculty needs a better capture. The reason is shown. |

**If resubmission is requested**, open the participation and capture again. This
creates a *new version* — your original submission is not deleted, and both
remain in the history. The same event-date rule applies to the new capture.

**If an HOD overrides your Faculty's decision**, both decisions stay visible,
and the override is the one that counts.

---

## Attendance and On-Duty

You do not request these yourself. Once your evidence is verified, your Faculty
requests attendance and/or on-duty on your behalf, and your HOD approves or
rejects.

**Attendance** and **OD** are independent: one can be approved and the other
rejected. Approving one never implies the other.

*My Attendance* and *My OD* show each request's status, who reviewed it, when,
and the reason for any rejection.

---

## Achievements

Achievements are created by Faculty (or HOD/Admin) — never by you. A Faculty
achievement goes to your HOD for approval.

*My Achievements* shows the title, type, date and status. **Only approved
achievements are official**; anything in draft or pending is not yet a record
you can cite.

---

## Notifications

The bell in the header shows your unread count, refreshed about once a minute.
Click a notification to go straight to what it is about.

You are notified when an event you can attend is published or cancelled, when
your evidence is verified, rejected or needs resubmission, when an HOD overrides
a decision, when attendance or OD is approved or rejected, and when an
achievement is created, approved or rejected.

**You only ever see your own notifications.** Nobody else can see yours.

---

## Analytics, reports and recommendations

**Analytics** shows your own participation, verification, attendance, OD and
achievement figures over time. You see only your own data.

**Reports** lets you export your participation, achievement, attendance and OD
records as **CSV**, **XLSX** or **PDF** — useful for a portfolio or a scholarship
application. Only your own records are included, whatever you ask for.

**Recommendations** suggests events currently open for registration, ranked by
similarity to what you have taken part in before, with a short reason for each.

> A recommendation score is a **similarity**, not a probability. It says "this
> looks like things you have done", not "you will attend this" or "you will do
> well". If you have no history yet, the suggestions are based on your
> department, current popularity and how soon the event is — and the app says so.

An **engagement label** (low / moderate / high) may also be shown. It describes
your activity counts relative to other students right now. **It is not a grade,
a ranking or an academic judgment**, and no decision about you is made from it.

---

## Good practice

- Capture at the event, not afterwards — the date rule is strict.
- Show the surroundings, not just your face.
- Check your accuracy reading before you shoot; move if it is poor.
- Submit while you are still at the venue if you can.
- Read rejection reasons — they tell you exactly what to fix.
- Log out on shared devices.
- Never share your password, and never ask someone else to capture for you. The
  photo, the location and the time are all recorded.
